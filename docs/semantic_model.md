## Model

`EV Respondents` is the fact. `Demographics`, `Income`, `Mobility`, `Charging`,
and `Attitude and Incentive` filter it through five dimension keys. Use dimension
attributes for slicers, and explicit measures for numeric summaries. Keys,
lineage, raw target flag and band sort columns are hidden. Age/income/commute
band labels are sorted by their numeric order columns.

There are 12 explicit measures in three folders:

- Purchase intent: Respondents, Intending to Buy EV, Not Intending to Buy EV,
  Purchase Intent Rate.
- Respondent profile: Average Age, Average Annual Income USD,
  Average Daily Commute km, Average Cars Owned.
- Charging and incentives: Home Charging Access Rate, Subsidy Availability Rate,
  Average Chargers Near Home, Average Chargers Near Work.

Purchase Intent Rate divides the filtered Yes count by the filtered respondent
count; an empty denominator returns blank. Home charging/subsidy measures filter
their respective dimensions and preserve current selections via KEEPFILTERS.

The two Gold aggregate tables are excluded from the semantic model; adding the
35 segment rows together would multiply respondents by ten. There is no date
dimension because the dataset has no business-event date. This synthetic train
sample measures purchase intent, not actual sales, revenue or causal effects.
